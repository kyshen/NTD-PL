\section{Conclusion}

This paper develops NTD-PL as a Tucker enhancement with a shared polynomial
link. The model keeps a Tucker latent tensor as the backbone, and polynomial
powers of that same latent tensor induce parameter-tied, shared-backbone rank
lifts rather than untied high-rank corrections. The analysis formalizes this
view through Veronese rank expansion, an expressivity gap over matched-rank
Tucker, and approximation results for nonlinear links. The optimizer combines
Link Refresh, Linked-Backbone Descent, and degree continuation, while the
experiments and diagnostics identify the scoped empirical regime: the link
helps when matched-rank Tucker leaves a coherent nonlinear residual explained
by a scalar response. Future work may consider adaptive links, scalable sparse
implementations, and richer structured responses.
