\section*{NeurIPS Paper Checklist}

This draft uses a working checklist. It should be replaced by the official
NeurIPS checklist distributed with the final 2026 submission template.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Claims.} The main claims are stated in the abstract,
  introduction, theory section, and experiment captions: NTD-PL is a
  parameter-efficient nonlinear observation model for Tucker tensors; its
  gains are not explained by modest rank increases or post-hoc scalar
  calibration.
  \item \textbf{Limitations.} Section~\ref{sec:limitations} discusses
  near-linear boundary cases, whole-axis missingness, shared-link assumptions,
  and the current domain concentration in hyperspectral data.
  \item \textbf{Theory assumptions.} The approximation results assume a
  bounded latent tensor and polynomial or analytic scalar links. The
  separation results use generic full-rank feature constructions. The masked
  generalization bound assumes bounded parameters, bounded observations, and
  random observed entries.
  \item \textbf{Reproducibility.} The experiment section reports datasets,
  ranks, mask types, missing rates, and baseline definitions. The codebase
  includes scripts for rank sweeps, MLPCal, structured missingness, and the
  cross-domain real-tensor reconstruction benchmark; the final version should add hardware
  details and exact command manifests.
\end{enumerate}
