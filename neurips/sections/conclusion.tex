\section{Conclusion}

We introduced NTD-PL, a Tucker enhancement with a shared polynomial observation
link. Rather than replacing multilinear low-rank structure, NTD-PL acts as a
shared-backbone rank lift: powers of one learned latent tensor express
structured residuals left by a linear Tucker fit without adding an independent
high-rank correction.

The experiments support the same scoped interpretation. Controlled tensors,
held-out CAVE completion, reconstruction mechanism checks, residual-link
diagnostics, and external HSI scenes show that the link helps when a shared
nonlinear residual is plausible under a tight rank budget, while also revealing
predicted near-linear boundary cases. Adaptive links and broader tensor
modalities are natural next steps.
