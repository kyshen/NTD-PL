\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

NTD-PL assumes that a common nonlinear response explains a coherent part of the
residual left by matched-rank Tucker. A single scalar link may be less
effective when nonlinearities vary strongly across spatial regions, spectral
bands, modes, or samples; when the remaining error is ordinary multilinear
structure rather than a scalar response, increasing Tucker rank remains the
more direct modeling choice.

Polynomial degree adds approximation capacity but can amplify Tucker scale
ambiguity and conditioning issues in the power basis, motivating the
ridge-regularized Link Refresh step, factor normalization, and degree
continuation. The joint problem also remains nonconvex: Link Refresh is a
small ridge regression with fixed latent tensor, but the backbone and link are
learned jointly, and we do not claim global optimality.

Our strongest empirical evidence is from controlled nonlinear residuals and
hyperspectral reconstruction and completion. Broader validation on large sparse
tensors, temporal or multimodal tensors, and domain-varying nonlinear
responses is left for future work.
