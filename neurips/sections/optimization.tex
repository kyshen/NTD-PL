\section{Optimization}
\label{sec:optimization}

We optimize the masked objective in Section~\ref{sec:masked-objective} with a
simple alternating scheme. The Tucker backbone \((\cX,\{\bA^{(n)}\})\) is
updated by first-order steps through the current polynomial link, while the
link coefficients are updated by a small ridge-regression problem on the
current latent entries. This gives a practical solver while keeping the
optimization layer standard.

Given the current latent tensor
\(\cS=\tucker{\cX}{\bA^{(1)},\ldots,\bA^{(N)}}\), the coefficient update for
active degree \(Q\) solves
\begin{equation}
  \min_{\bbeta_{0:Q}}
  \frac{1}{2|\Omega|}
  \norm{\by_\Omega-\bPhi_{\Omega,Q}(\cS)\bbeta_{0:Q}}_2^2
  +
  \frac{\lambda_\beta}{2}\norm{\bbeta_{0:Q}}_2^2,
  \label{eq:main-beta-subproblem}
\end{equation}
where \(\bPhi_{\Omega,Q}(\cS)\) contains the observed powers
\([1,s,\ldots,s^Q]\). The backbone update uses the chain rule through
\(f_{\bbeta}\); the implementation evaluates \(f_{\bbeta}(\cS)\) and
\(f_{\bbeta}'(\cS)\) together, then backpropagates the masked residual through
the Tucker reconstruction.

Training starts from the Tucker-compatible degree-one model and activates
higher powers gradually. When a new degree is activated, its coefficient is
initialized at zero, so the model expands from the current low-degree fit
rather than beginning with all nonlinear terms active. We also normalize factor
scales and absorb them into the core, which fixes a Tucker gauge without
changing \(\cS\).

The dominant cost is the same forward and reverse Tucker contractions used by
standard Tucker optimization at the chosen rank. The polynomial link adds
\(O(P|\Omega|)\) elementwise work and a \((P+1)\times(P+1)\) ridge solve, so
for the small degrees used here its overhead is minor. Full update equations,
stabilization details, and pseudocode are given in
Appendix~\ref{app:optimization-details}.
